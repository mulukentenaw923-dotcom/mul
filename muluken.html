Welcome to Mekdela Amba University

Mekdela Amba University (MAU) is a leading public higher education institution in Ethiopia, dedicated to academic excellence, research, and community service. Established to foster innovation and knowledge, MAU offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs across various disciplines. The university is committed to nurturing competent graduates who contribute to national development and global progress.

  • Modern Campus: MAU boasts state-of-the-art facilities, libraries, laboratories, and a vibrant campus life.
  • Quality Education: Our experienced faculty and diverse academic programs ensure students receive a world-class education.
  • Research & Innovation: The university encourages research that addresses local and global challenges.
  • Community Engagement: MAU is dedicated to serving society through outreach, partnerships, and social responsibility initiatives.

Join us at Mekdela Amba University and be part of a dynamic academic community shaping the future of Ethiopia and beyond.

About the System

This system allows Mekdela Amba University (MAU) students to conveniently check their academic results, including individual course grades, overall Grade Point Average (GPA), and academic standing (pass/fail status), by simply entering their unique student ID.

students view grade parctice

Services

  • Secure viewing of individual course grades based on Student ID.
  • Automatic calculation and display of Grade Point Average (GPA).
  • Conversion of numerical grades to letter grades (e.g., 4.0 to A+).
  • Clear determination and display of academic status (Passed/Failed).
  • Option to print the displayed grade report.
  • Option to download the grade report as a PDF file.
  • (For Admins) Ability to add new student records, including their grades for predefined courses.
